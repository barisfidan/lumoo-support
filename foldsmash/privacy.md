# Fold Smash Support

Fold Smash is a pencil-and-paper battle for two players on one iPhone.

## Contact
Questions, feedback or bug reports: **barisfidann3@gmail.com**

## FAQ

### How do I play?
Each player places up to three stick figures on their own page, then puts a target on the mirror spot of an opponent's figure. Fold the book to see whose marks matched. Matched figures raise a white flag; the last side standing wins.

### Do I need an iPhone Duo?
No. On iPhone Duo you close the phone itself to fold the book. On other iPhones, tap **Fold** and then **Open**.

### Does the app collect any data?
No. See the [privacy policy](privacy).
